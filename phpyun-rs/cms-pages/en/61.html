PHPYUN is a leading recruitment system in China. We work to support social progress and business growth. We listen to our customers, protect every user's rights, and value your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Non-personal information

We generally collect non-personal information, such as which PHPYUN pages you visit most often, which services you use most, and aggregated employment statistics that may be published. We use this data in aggregate form. It helps us understand what is most useful, improve our products and operations, create a better experience, and more accurately prevent phishing sites and malware. We may share this non-personal information with business partners, but only in aggregate form so they can also improve the environment for you.

Personal information

We typically collect basic personal information (name, gender, date of birth, mobile number, email, work experience, years of experience, expected occupation, expected work location, and similar fields) for recruitment purposes (job seekers finding work and employers finding candidates). With your consent, PHPYUN (including us, our affiliates, or delegated third parties) may use this information to send job recommendations, product or service information that may interest you, risk notices, security verification, or notices when PHPYUN changes.

If you join a campaign, use an interactive area, or purchase a product or online/offline service listed on PHPYUN, we may need additional profile information and photos. That data is used to help you submit higher-quality content and take part in those activities or services.

We may also run surveys (topics of interest, sites you like or dislike, careers of interest, and similar questions) so we can improve our services and the online environment.

When using PHPYUN services, please do not submit sensitive personal information that is not required for the service (such as race, religion, or political views). Such information could be collected accidentally.

After we collect personal information, we may de-identify it with technical means so it can no longer identify an individual. We may use de-identified data and, without disclosing your personal information, analyze the user database for commercial purposes. Unless you delete the data or refuse collection through system settings, the personal information you provide while using our products and/or services remains authorized for our use during that period. After you close your account, we will handle your personal information as required by law.

Disclosure of information

Except for the non-personal information described above, we will not disclose your PHPYUN browsing activity or the personal data you give us (such as name, address, email, phone number, payment card number, or photos), unless:

(1) we have obtained your prior consent or authorization;

(2) disclosure is required by law, regulation, a court or regulator, a government or supervisory request, or PHPYUN reasonably believes it is needed to meet a regulatory obligation;

(3) it is necessary to protect you from an imminent risk to life, body, or property;

(4) sharing is necessary to provide the service you requested (including products from affiliates and partners) or to handle a dispute between you and another party;

(5) disclosure is allowed under an agreement you signed with us (including online terms and platform rules) or another legal document;

(6) it is needed to complete a merger, split, acquisition, or asset transfer;

(7) it is needed to protect the lawful rights of PHPYUN, its affiliates, or partners;

(8) it is used for a public interest recognized by law.

If you enter personal data on a page jointly run with a partner (for example a co-branded campaign), that data belongs to both PHPYUN and the partner. PHPYUN is not liable for how the partner then uses that personal information.

PHPYUN may share your personal information with affiliates, but only as needed and only for the purposes stated in this policy.

Please think carefully about information you upload, publish, or exchange through our services. In some cases you can use privacy settings to control who may see shared information.

(2) a force-majeure event causes a network outage and service interruption;

By placing your resume in our database you acknowledge these risks and agree to bear them. PHPYUN is not liable for related disputes.

Use and storage

Information we collect is mainly used to provide, maintain, and improve our services.

We may contact you through the details you provided, for example about your account, security updates, or product and service notices.

We may also recommend content that may interest you. If you are not interested, you can reply to cancel those notices.

Information collected about you is stored on servers of PHPYUN and/or its affiliates. It may be transferred to, accessed from, stored in, or displayed in your country or region, or in the place where it was collected.

While your account is active or we still need to provide the service, we retain the personal data you provided. Even if you only use the service every few years to look for a new job, we keep your information and professional profile available until you close the account. In some cases we retain certain information in a non-identifying or aggregated form.

If you close your account, your personal data is usually no longer shown to other users of the service. We generally delete closed-account information except where we must keep some data to meet legal or regulatory duties, respond to law-enforcement requests, resolve disputes, keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce the user agreement, or honor an unsubscribe request. After the account is closed we may retain non-identifying information.

Information you have already shared with others (for example by email, a feed, or a group) may remain visible after you close the account or delete it from your profile or inbox, and we cannot control copies other users made from the service. Group content tied to a closed account may appear as coming from an unknown user. Your professional profile may still appear on other parties' services until they refresh their cache.

Protection of personal information

We retain your personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy and as required by law. To protect your information we use reasonable physical, electronic, and administrative measures so that it is not disclosed, damaged, or lost, including SSL, encrypted storage, and data-center access control. Staff or contractors who may access your information are strictly managed, including role-based permissions, confidentiality agreements, and operation monitoring. PHPYUN uses current technology to provide a reasonable level of protection and will work to prevent disclosure, damage, or loss.

Your account has security features. Please keep your account and password safe. PHPYUN backs up data to other servers and encrypts passwords to reduce the risk of loss, abuse, or alteration. Even with these measures, no network system is perfectly secure. If your account or password is leaked through your own actions, you are responsible for the legal consequences.

When you use PHPYUN services for online transactions you may need to give the other party personal details such as a contact name or phone number. Protect that information and share it only when necessary. If you believe your personal information has been leaked or may be leaked, and that this could threaten your PHPYUN account or cause other loss, notify PHPYUN customer service at once so we can help protect the account and limit further harm. If you do not notify us promptly, you bear the resulting loss (including any increase in loss).
