Member tasks are assignments published in the member center. Completing them earns experience toward a higher member level and more redemption rights. Open the member center, open the task page, and follow each task's rules.

Member level rises as you earn experience by using the product (for example by signing in). Higher levels unlock extras.

Member privileges may include more mall items, lower redemption prices, exclusive skins, beta access, and prize draws. Keep completing tasks to unlock more.

Member coins are a virtual currency for redemption. Coins are currently earned mainly by checking in at the member mall.
