Package notes:

1. Annual packages last one year for every employer.

2. "Live jobs" means how many jobs can be shown at the same time. Jobs that are not live do not count.

3. "Resumes" means resumes the employer may download (not including applications sent by candidates). Daily and weekly download caps apply; the package states the total. Searching resumes is unlimited.

4. "Multi-company management" means how many subsidiaries the employer may set up and manage.

5. "On-site booths" means discounts at comprehensive job fairs run by the talent market.

6. Membership fees already paid are not refunded if the member resigns during the year.

7. Packages at or above the stated amount may include a training discount while that campaign is active.
