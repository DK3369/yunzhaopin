Promotion services let employers and individuals buy extra visibility. Employers can purchase ad slots, job bidding, urgent hiring, value packs, and membership packages. For faster hiring, consider a VIP upgrade for more display opportunities.

1. Employer services

Scheduled auto-refresh. Buy auto-refresh so jobs are refreshed on a 1 / 3 / 15 / 30 day (or custom) cycle without logging in every day. The system runs it from the next day.

Flow: register as an employer → upload a business license → post a job → top up → job management → promote → scheduled auto-refresh.

Job bidding. Bid so your job ranks higher. Rank depends on your bid versus others in the same category (credits per day).

Featured jobs. Featured jobs appear on the home page, the job list, and the mobile site for 1 / 3 / 15 / 30 days (or a custom period) and take effect at once.

Urgent hiring. Urgent jobs appear on the home page and the job list for a chosen period and take effect at once.

2. Ad slots

Employers can buy ad slots themselves. After customer service approves the order, the ad goes live.
