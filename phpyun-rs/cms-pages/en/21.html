This site is a human-resources platform operated by the company behind this recruitment network. Services include online hiring, on-site hiring, executive search, vocational training, corporate training, and talent assessment. After years of growth the platform has served tens of thousands of employers and become a primary choice for many companies and job seekers. We provide career information and opportunities across a professional lifetime.

1. Service network

Based in Jiangsu and covering East China, the network has grown from a single Nanjing site to city sites and local offices in Nanjing, Suzhou, Wuxi, Huaian, and other cities.

2. Strengths

1. High traffic and strong results.

2. Hire anytime, without limits of time or place.

3. Search a leading local resume database.

4. Professional human customer service.

5. Dedicated staff track hiring results and give advisory service.

6. A large number of people look for jobs on the site at any moment.

3. Services for employers

Job posting: log in with your registered account and publish the latest openings to attract applicants.

Resume search: members can search the latest talent data by their own criteria and pick matching resumes.

Application management: members can sort received resumes and record comments.

Logo / banner links: if you want a stronger brand presence, you can use image links (Logo, Banner) so more candidates understand your company.

4. Services for individuals

Job-seeker services are free.

You can manage your resume with your account, including photos and certificate images.

You can apply online and track applications in your member center.

You can see which employers viewed your resume and follow up on opportunities.
