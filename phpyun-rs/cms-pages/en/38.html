A. Questions from employers

1. What are promotion services?
Promotion services are paid extras that put your information in a more visible place on the category list. Views are often several times higher than a normal post, but results still depend on how complete and truthful your content and images are.

2. Assign someone to the account
Give the hiring account to a dedicated colleague. If that person leaves, hand over the username and password and change the password. If nobody logs in, you will not receive calls or resumes.

3. Sign in often
Signing in refreshes your company and jobs on the home page and updates the post date, so more candidates can find you.

4. Where do featured / urgent / recommended jobs appear?
Headhunting and similar packages can appear in brand positions on the home page. Graphic urgent hiring and home-page recommendations appear on the home page and in latest jobs. Order follows the start time of the promotion; the newest items sit at the top.

5. How do I buy urgent, featured, or ad-slot services?
Register as an employer, complete and publish a job, wait for review, top up site credits, then buy the service.

6. How is display time and order decided?
Display time follows the period you bought. Order follows when the promotion started. Renewing moves the item back to the top; leftover days are not added together.

7. Write a complete company profile and job description
Incomplete posts look less serious. Describe the work, team, and requirements honestly so the right people apply.

8. Reply to resumes and messages quickly
Check applications every day. Decline politely when the resume is not a fit, and close jobs that are already filled.

9. Review standards
No pornographic, illegal, or false information. The company name must match the business license. Do not put a phone number or email in the job text. Do not add wording that pretends the site has certified the post.

10. Payment
Alipay (including most Chinese online-banking options) is supported; more methods may be added later.
